Your Smooth 4-Day London Itinerary

A seamless plan exploring your chosen highlights, grouped by location.

Day 1: Royal & Political Heart

This section covers the iconic landmarks of Westminster and the South Bank. These sites are all within walking distance, making for a smooth and memorable first day exploring the heart of Royal London.

Morning

Buckingham Palace

Start your day at the Queen's official residence. Arrive around 10:30 AM to see the Changing of the Guard (on select days, check schedule).

➡️ Next: Enjoy a beautiful walk through St. James's Park or Green Park towards Westminster.

Late Morning

Westminster Abbey & Big Ben

Book tickets in advance for the Abbey, a stunning Gothic church and site of royal coronations. Afterwards, see the iconic Big Ben and the Houses of Parliament from Parliament Square.

➡️ Next: Walk across Westminster Bridge for the best photos.

Afternoon

London Eye

Head to the South Bank for your pre-booked slot on the London Eye. This giant observation wheel offers breathtaking 360-degree views of the entire city.

➡️ Next: The pier for the river cruise is right nearby.

Evening

Cruise down River Thames

A perfect way to end the day. A sunset or evening cruise gives you a new perspective on the illuminated landmarks you saw earlier, from the water.

Day 2: Historic Southwark & Culture

Today explores 1,000 years of history at the Tower, the vibrant tastes of London's most famous food market, and one of the world's greatest museums. This route requires one short Tube ride in the afternoon.

Morning

Tower of London

Arrive at opening (10:00 AM) to beat the crowds. See the Crown Jewels, meet the famous Beefeaters, and learn about its history as a palace, prison, and zoo.

➡️ Next: It's a short walk to London Bridge and Borough Market.

Lunchtime

Borough Market & London Bridge

Explore London's premier food market, located right by London Bridge. This is the perfect spot for lunch, with endless options from global street food to classic British pies.

➡️ Next: Hop on the Northern line tube from London Bridge to Tottenham Court Road.

Afternoon

British Museum

Home to a vast collection of world art and artifacts. Entry is free. Don't miss the Rosetta Stone, the Parthenon sculptures, and the stunning Great Court.

Day 3: Museums, Parks & Palaces

This day is focused on the beautiful South Kensington and Knightsbridge area. You'll explore a world-class museum, relax in London's most famous park, and visit the city's most iconic luxury department store.

Morning

Natural History Museum

Start your day in South Kensington at this breathtaking museum (entry is free, but book a time slot). Famous for its dinosaur skeletons and the magnificent Hintze Hall.

➡️ Next: The museum is right next to Hyde Park.

Midday

Hyde Park

Enjoy a refreshing walk through this massive, beautiful park. You can walk along the Serpentine lake, see the Diana Memorial Fountain, or just relax on the grass.

➡️ Next: A short walk from the south side of the park takes you to Harrods.

Afternoon

Harrods for Shopping

Experience the world's most famous luxury department store. Even if you're just browsing, the opulent Food Halls and the Egyptian Escalator are sights to behold.

Day 4: Markets & Shopping

A day dedicated to London's diverse shopping and market culture. From the colorful streets of Notting Hill to the alternative vibe of Camden, finishing at the world's most famous shopping street.

Morning

Notting Hill

Explore this charming West London neighborhood. See the colorful houses and browse the antique and fashion stalls at Portobello Road Market (main market day is Saturday).

➡️ Next: Take the Central line tube to Camden Town (change at Tottenham Court Road).

Lunch & Afternoon

Camden Market

A vast, eclectic market known for its alternative fashion, unique crafts, and amazing global street food. This is the perfect place to grab lunch and explore.

➡️ Next: Take the Northern line tube south to Oxford Circus.

Late Afternoon

Oxford Street

End your shopping day on Europe's busiest shopping street, home to flagship stores for countless major brands. Don't miss Selfridges department store.

⚠️ A Note on Harry Potter Studios

This is a vital part of your plan! The Warner Bros. Studio Tour London is not in central London. It is located in Watford Junction, which is about an hour away by train and shuttle bus.

A visit to the studios is a fantastic experience, but it is an all-day event. It typically takes 5-7 hours including travel time.

You cannot combine the Studio Tour with any of the other days. You must dedicate one of your 4 days entirely to it.

Recommendation: If the Harry Potter Studios is a top priority, please substitute it for one of the other 4 days (Day 3 or Day 4 would be the easiest to swap out).
